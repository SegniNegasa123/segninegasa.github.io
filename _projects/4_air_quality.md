    <div class="dossier-bio-narrative">
      <p>Conducted collaborative computational research investigating aerosol dispersion patterns, particulate matter (PM2.5) concentrations, and meteorological feedback loops in rapidly urbanizing African metropolises. Mentored by research professors from UNC Chapel Hill and North Carolina A&T State University.</p>

  <section class="dossier-section">
    <div class="dossier-section-header">
      <h3 class="dossier-section-title">Technical Architecture & Engineering</h3>
      <span class="dossier-section-tag">[ARCHITECTURE]</span>
    </div>
    <div class="dossier-bio-narrative">
- **Satellite & Sensor Fusion**: Ingestion of multi-spectral Sentinel-5P atmospheric observations correlated with localized low-cost ground IoT air quality monitors.
- **Temporal Feature Engineering**: Modeled diurnal thermal inversions, wind velocity gradients, and unpaved road dust resuspension indices.
- **Ensemble Gradient Boosting**: Extreme Gradient Boosting (XGBoost) and Spatial Graph Neural Networks predicting 24-hour pollution spikes with R² > 0.89.
- **Public Health Risk Mapping**: High-resolution heatmaps identifying vulnerable neighborhoods and hospital zones exposed to hazardous AQI levels.

    </div>
