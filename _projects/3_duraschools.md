    <div class="dossier-section-header">
      <h3 class="dossier-section-title">Technical Architecture & Engineering</h3>
      <span class="dossier-section-tag">[ARCHITECTURE]</span>
    </div>
    <div class="dossier-bio-narrative">
- **Fault-Tolerant Hybrid Architecture**: Offline-first synchronized local cache syncing with central PostgreSQL cloud databases during intermittent connectivity windows.
- **Multi-Tenant Role RBAC**: Secure credentialing system separating regional bureau auditors, school principals, teachers, parents, and students.
- **Automated Grading & Transcript Engine**: Generates ministry-compliant transcript dossiers and performance analytics in under 5 seconds.
- **SMS & Micro-Payment Gateway**: Automated grade alerts and digital tuition processing via Telebirr and local banking APIs.
