    <div class="dossier-bio-narrative">
      <p>
        Growing up in Nekemte, Ethiopia, I witnessed a society bottlenecked by manual, paper-based systems. I realized early on that engineering is not merely an academic exercise—it is a tool for survival and systemic restoration. This conviction deepened when severe regional conflict forced my family into displacement, fundamentally rewiring my approach to technology from building prototypes to engineering digital lifelines.
      </p>
      <p>
        Today, I bridge the gap between theoretical machine learning and production-grade civic infrastructure. As the Founder of DURA Tech, I deployed DuraSchools, an EdTech SaaS platform scaling digital education across Ethiopia. In AI research, I am the lead architect of Tereguwami, a Ministry of Innovation-backed, 9-layer multimodal AI system bridging communication for over 4 million Ethiopian Sign Language users through generative avatars and sEMG "silent speech" interfaces.
      </p>
      <p>
        My applied work is backed by rigorous theoretical training, including specialized AI/ML certification from the Ethiopian Gifted and Talented Education (EGATE) program, a top 0.5% Evangadi Full-Stack Fellowship, and computational research on urban air quality modeling with professors from UNC Chapel Hill and NC A&T State University.
      </p>
    </div>
