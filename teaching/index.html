My approach to technical pedagogy bridges theoretical computer science with production-grade civic software deployment. Project Coordinator & Teaching Assistant (AI/ML)

Ethiopian Gifted and Talented Education (EGATE) — Cohort 2
2026 – Present // Addis Ababa, ET

After graduating at the top of Cohort 1, I was recruited to the instructional faculty for EGATE Cohort 2. I co-designed and delivered an advanced machine learning and software engineering curriculum for 16 specialized national scholar students, taking them from core foundational mathematics to containerized production deployment.

Key Instructional Domains & Deliverables:

  • Deep Learning Architectures: Delivered in-depth lectures on Convolutional Networks, Spatio-Temporal Graph Convolutional Networks (ST-GCNs), and Attention mechanisms using PyTorch.
  • Production MLOps: Trained students on experiment tracking, model quantization via ONNX Mobile, and serialization pipelines for resource-constrained edge hardware.
  • Deployment Engineering: Coached fellows on packaging AI inference models inside Docker containers and exposing clean REST API contracts with Flask and FastAPI.
  • Capstone Mentorship: Directed 16 individual student teams developing applied AI capstone prototypes tackling civic, medical, and agricultural problems.

Founder & Technical Instructor

Digital Bridge Initiative Club — ODA Special Boarding School
2024 – 2025 // Adama, ET

Recognizing that students at one of Ethiopia’s top boarding schools lacked hands-on production engineering experience, I founded the Digital Bridge Initiative Club. I established an extracurricular software engineering academy, teaching full-stack web development and guiding members to engineer production software for their own school.

Key Instructional Domains & Deliverables:

  • Full-Stack Engineering: Taught weekly intensive workshops covering semantic HTML/CSS, modern JavaScript, Python backends, and relational database schema design.
  • Architectural Stewardship: Led student engineers through sprint planning, version control (Git/GitHub), code reviews, and software testing lifecycles.
  • Production Deployment — ODA VIEW: Guided the student engineering team to design, build, and deploy ODA VIEW, a comprehensive school management, student grading, and library portal.
  • Measurable Impact: The system was officially adopted by the school administration, directly serving 1,000+ active students, teachers, and administrators.

Founder & Lead Instructor

Nekemte Summer Coding Camp — Grassroots Computational Literacy
Summers 2024 & 2025 // Nekemte, Western Oromia, ET

Motivated by my roots in Western Ethiopia where access to modern computing labs and technical instructors is severely constrained, I founded the Nekemte Summer Coding Camp. The initiative was built from the ground up to introduce computational thinking, algorithmic logic, and foundational programming to underprivileged rural youths.

Key Instructional Domains & Deliverables:

  • Computational Thinking: Introduced procedural problem solving, pseudocode design, conditional branching, and control structures to first-time computer users.
  • Offline-First Pedagogy: Developed offline-compatible lesson modules and interactive terminal exercises tailored for environments experiencing frequent grid outages and intermittent internet access.
  • Applied Python Fundamentals: Guided students through hands-on scripting projects, arithmetic calculators, text adventure engines, and data structures.
  • Empowerment & Retention: Trained 45+ rural high school students, with several participants going on to pursue computer science and national science competition projects.
